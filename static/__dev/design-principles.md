ONIDUKA MINI - DESIGN PRINCIPLES

DISTINCTIVE THREAD
"Painted signboard, set true." Nigerian roadside shop signage is bold, confident and legible from across the street. Oniduka renders that confidence with print discipline instead of pastiche: heavy optical display type, a warm painted-bone ground, one deep forest-green ink, one ochre signal. Flat painted surfaces, hairline rules, almost no shadow. The result should read as a small business that paid a good designer, not as a dashboard and not as a template.

MODES
Both. Light is the primary experience and the default for public store pages. Dark is fully supported for the owner dashboard.

PACING REGISTER
   10	Split. Public store pages are generous: wide gutters, large product imagery, room between sections, type that is comfortable at arm's length on a phone. The owner dashboard is tight: compact rows, dense tables, small controls, no giant stat cards.

   12	GLOBAL CHARACTER
   13	Surfaces are flat and painted. Cards sit on a tinted bone surface that is visibly warmer than the page ground; they are separated by hairline borders and a barely-there shadow, never by heavy elevation. Radius is modest and consistent - signboard corners, not pills - except for avatars, logo chips and status badges which go fully round.
   14	Green is structural: primary actions, active nav, the brand mark. Ochre is a signal only - featured products, prices that need weight, a pending count. Never both saturated in the same block.
   15	Display face is Bricolage Grotesque, used heavy and tight for business names, page titles and section heads; it carries all the personality. Body and UI face is Inter Tight, kept quiet at normal weight. Money and quantities use IBM Plex Mono with tabular figures so naira amounts line up in carts, order lists and product tables.
   16	Imagery does the selling on public pages; chrome stays out of the way. Empty states are written as full sentences with one clear action, never as a shrug.
   17	No gradients. No decorative animation - motion is limited to state feedback under 200ms.
